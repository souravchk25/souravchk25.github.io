What if only some arms are open each round, and which ones depends on the arm you just pulled?

A classic bandit can pull any arm in any round. A scout robot looking for the best place to put a radio relay after a storm cannot: it can check only the sites it can drive to from where it is, and streets close and reopen. The same happens when a data collector moves through a sensor network, or a patrol follows paths that open and close. The learner sees the reward only of the arm it pulls, so exploring means moving, and once the best arm is known it can still be out of reach for a while.
