I am a [Computer Science](https://www.colorado.edu/cs/) Ph.D. candidate at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/), advised by Prof. [Lijun Chen](https://spot.colorado.edu/~lich1539/). In 2026 I also spent time as a Visiting Student Researcher in [Computing and Mathematical Sciences at Caltech](https://www.cms.caltech.edu/), working with Profs. [Steven Low](http://netlab.caltech.edu/) and [Adam Wierman](https://adamwierman.com/).

My research is on the mathematical foundations of sequential decision-making under uncertainty, mainly **multi-armed bandits**<a class="jump" href="#research" aria-label="Go to Research" title="Go to Research">↓</a> (classical and quantum<a class="jump" href="#t-quantum" aria-label="Go to quantum bandits" title="Go to quantum bandits">↓</a>) and **multi-agent reinforcement learning**<a class="jump" href="#t-marl" aria-label="Go to multi-agent reinforcement learning" title="Go to multi-agent reinforcement learning">↓</a>: decentralized coordination, continuous actions, and evolving networks. More recently I have turned to **large language models**<a class="jump" href="#t-fm" aria-label="Go to large language models" title="Go to large language models">↓</a>: the **geometry of attention**, and **reinforcement learning post-training** for **reasoning** and **alignment**.

Earlier, I earned my bachelor's degree from the [Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra](https://www.bitmesra.ac.in/) and worked as a Software Engineer at [Flipkart](https://www.linkedin.com/company/flipkart/) in Bangalore, designing scalable search pipelines and data recovery platforms.
